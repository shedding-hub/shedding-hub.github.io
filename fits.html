Fitted curves

Rise-and-decay models fitted to every analyte with enough data, with the parameters a transmission or wastewater model consumes.

How to read this

What is in the table

One row per fitted analyte. Each fit is a censored maximum-likelihood fit of a shedding curve to one biomarker in one specimen from one study, so the same study appears once per analyte and once per model.

  • Peak is the population mean peak concentration, log10 of the analyte's own unit.
  • σ is the residual scale — larger means the individual measurements scatter further from the fitted curve.
  • Limit is the censoring limit, below which a measurement counts as a non-detect rather than a number.
  • AIC compares models fitted to the same analyte. Lower is better. It is meaningless across different analytes.

To see a fit plotted against the underlying measurements, open the study with Explore dataset from the datasets page.
